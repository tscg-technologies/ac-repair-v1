Symptom Diagnostics

Common Air Conditioning Problems

Recognize the early signs of cooling dysfunction. Understanding common symptoms helps identify the right assessment category before small issues escalate.

AC Not Cooling Properly

Air blowing from the vent feels room temperature or lukewarm, pointing to clogged coils, sensor faults, or pressure imbalances.

Request Assessment

AC Not Turning On

The unit fails to respond to power switches or remotes, often indicating capacitor failure, wiring disconnection, or circuit trip.

Request Assessment

Water Leakage From Indoor Unit

Water dripping down interior walls usually indicates a blocked condensate drainage tray, algae accumulation, or improper slope.

Request Assessment

Unusual Noise or Vibration

Squealing, grinding, or loud rattling sounds commonly stem from loose motor bearings, unbalanced blower wheels, or loose casing.

Request Assessment

Weak Airflow

Airflow volume feels restricted even on high fan settings, indicating heavily clogged air filters, iced coils, or blower motor wear.

Request Assessment

Unpleasant Odour

Musty, stale or damp smells upon starting the AC typically indicate bacterial growth in the condensate pan or dirty cooling fins.

Request Assessment

Outdoor Unit Problems

The outdoor condenser fan fails to rotate, makes excessive noise, or shuts down prematurely due to overheating or debris blockage.

Request Assessment

Thermostat or Remote Issues

Inaccurate ambient temperature readings, uncalibrated setpoints, or unresponsive IR receivers causing erratic cycle switching.

Request Assessment

Safety Notice: Air conditioning units contain high-voltage electrical circuits and pressurized refrigerant lines. Never attempt do-it-yourself electrical or refrigerant interventions.
