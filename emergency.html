Urgent Service Evaluation

AC NOT WORKING? LET'S IDENTIFY THE PROBLEM.

Unexpected air-conditioning problems can affect your comfort. Understand common AC issues and request a professional assessment.

Critical Failure Signs

Urgent AC Problems

If your air conditioner exhibits any of these symptoms, turn off the system to prevent further damage and request a technical assessment.

AC Suddenly Stops Cooling

Blower fan blows warm room air without temperature drop, indicating compressor trip, capacitor fault, or sudden pressure loss.

AC Completely Stops Working

System exhibits no lights, no display, and no reaction to remote or manual reset switches. May indicate circuit fuse or board shutdown.

Water Leaking From Indoor Unit

Active overflow cascading down interior walls and electrical sockets due to an obstructed drain or severely iced coil thaw.

Burning Smell From AC Equipment

Acrid, electrical, or melted plastic odours emanating from indoor vents or outdoor casing. Power off immediately at main switch.

Unusual Sounds or Vibration

Loud banging, grinding metallic noises, or severe chassis shaking caused by loose fan blades or seized compressor bearings.

Electrical Breaker Repeatedly Trips

MCB or safety fuse flips whenever AC turns on, signifying a short circuit, locked rotor amperage surge, or ground fault.

Outdoor Unit Not Operating

Indoor blower runs but the outdoor condenser fan and compressor remain totally silent, halting the refrigeration cycle.

Water Leakage Near Electricals

Drainage runoff pooling directly over switchboards, distribution boards, or wall plugs. Presents extreme electrical shock hazard.

Crucial Safety Guidance For Occupants

Please review these precautionary measures before requesting technician assistance:

  • If there is smoke, a burning smell, or suspected electrical danger, immediately power down the unit at the main isolator or circuit breaker if safe to do so.
  • Never touch exposed wiring, loose copper connections, or damaged electrical terminals.
  • Do not open electrical cover panels or attempt internal circuitry repairs without appropriate professional tools.
  • Keep pets, children, and electrical appliances clear of any equipment affected by water leakage.
  • In the event of an electrical fire or active sparks, seek immediate civic emergency assistance (fire & rescue services).
  • Do not attempt to touch, vent, recharge, or handle pressurized refrigerant components yourself.
